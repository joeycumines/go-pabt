What you're watching

Three refrigerated trucks (the colored rectangles labeled T1, T2, T3) must pick up cold cargo containers and deliver them to powered parking spots before time runs out.

The places

  • STAGING — the loading dock. Everything starts here, like a warehouse parking lot.
  • WEST / EAST — waypoints along the route, like intersections or rest stops.
  • BAY — the transfer station. Only one truck at a time, like a single-lane car wash.
  • SLOT-1/2/3 — the destinations. Each is a powered parking spot with a plug, like an RV campground hookup. The cargo must stay cold, so it needs a powered slot.

The cargo

Each container shows a temperature badge. Blue means deep freeze (−22°C, like your home freezer). Orange means chilled (−8°C, like your fridge). Slots have minimum temperature ratings — cargo must go somewhere cold enough.

The problems

  • Wind lockout — a lane (road) closes, like a highway shut down in a storm. Trucks must reroute.
  • Bay contention — two trucks want the BAY at once. One gets turned away, like a full drive-through.
  • Power rejection — a truck arrives at a slot but the power is out, like plugging in an RV to a dead outlet. The truck must go elsewhere.
  • RFID scan — a cargo's tracking tag gets read, like scanning a package barcode.

The goal

Get all three cold containers from STAGING to their powered slots before the cutoff (round 120). Watch the trucks navigate, deal with closures, and deliver.
